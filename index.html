If something goes wrong

What happens in an emergency

If we have a concern about your pet but don’t believe it’s life-threatening, we’ll reach out to you first so we can decide together whether they should see a vet.

If we believe your pet’s life is in danger, getting them seen comes first. We’re trained in pet first aid and will do what we can to keep them stable, and we’ll call you on the way. If two of us are there, one of us calls you right away.

Where we’d take them

When there’s time, we’ll call around for the closest vet that can see your pet, which can also save you money. When every minute counts, this is our plan:

Dogs & cats
  1. Your vet, if they’re open & can see your pet
  2. Mission Vet Urgent Care
  3. MedVet Cincinnati

Late at night, we go straight to MedVet Cincinnati.

Birds, small animals, reptiles and amphibians
  1. Your vet, if they’re open & can see your pet
  2. VEG Cincinnati in Oakley, open 24/7 & sees exotic pets
Fish

If something’s wrong with the tank or a fish looks sick, we’ll contact you right away.
